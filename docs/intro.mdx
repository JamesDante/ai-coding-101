# Welcome to AI Coding 101

> Learn how modern software is built with AI.

![Musical Clock with Spinet and Organ, ca. 1625 — Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/mi/web-large/DP302691.jpg)

---

AI is transforming the way we write code. Today, developers are no longer just typing lines of code—they are designing systems that **collaborate with AI agents**, orchestrate multiple models, and automate large portions of the software development workflow.

This course is designed for **developers and engineers** who want to understand the principles behind AI-assisted development, rather than just following tool-specific tutorials. By the end of AI Coding 101, you will be able to:

- Write **effective prompts** that guide AI to generate reliable code  
- Engineer **context and memory** for large codebases and complex tasks  
- Build **AI-powered agents** that can automate coding, testing, and deployment  
- Understand the **architecture behind coding agents**, such as Claude Code, multi-agent systems, and RAG pipelines  

We focus on **foundations and workflows that last**—not the fleeting features of any single AI tool. The knowledge gained here will remain relevant as AI continues to evolve over the next 5–10 years.

---

**How to use this course**

- Start with **Foundations** to understand how AI changes software development  
- Move on to **Prompt Engineering** and **Context Engineering** to master reliable AI outputs  
- Explore **AI-Assisted Development** to apply AI to real projects  
- Dive into **AI Agents** to understand multi-agent systems, tool-calling, and coding agent architecture  
- Finally, tackle the **Graduation Project** to build your own AI coding agent from scratch

Welcome to the future of programming. Let's get started!
